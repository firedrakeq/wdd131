Enugu Trails & Heritage Guide

Website Planning Document | Quentin Chime | WDD 131

1. Site Name

Enugu Trails — This name was chosen because Enugu is famously known as the "Coal City" and is surrounded by rolling hills, waterfalls, and historic landmarks. "Enugu Trails" captures both outdoor eco-tourism adventures and cultural heritage routes in a short, memorable domain-ready name.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Enugu Trails is to provide residents, students, and visitors with a reliable digital guide to Enugu State's top natural attractions, cultural sites, and local culinary spots. The website will feature an interactive destination filter (by category and budget), practical visitor tips, a trip cost estimator, and a contact/trip-inquiry form.

3. Scenarios

  • Which waterfalls, caves, or hills in Enugu (such as Awhum Waterfall or Ngwo Pine Forest) are open for weekend visits, and what are their entry fees?
  • Where can a first-time visitor find authentic local Enugu dishes like Okpa and Abacha near the city center?
  • How much should a small group budget for a one-day guided nature tour around Enugu?

4. Color Scheme

The palette reflects Enugu's lush green hills and warm terracotta earth tones while maintaining strong AA/AAA contrast:

Primary Forest Green (#1B4332) Used for the main header, footer, primary buttons, and section headings.
Terracotta Gold (#B45309) Used for interactive highlights, category badges, and accent borders.
Soft Mint Surface (#F0FDF4) Used for card backgrounds and callout panels against a crisp white (#FFFFFF) page background.

5. Typography

  • Merriweather (Serif): Applied to all main headings (h1, h2, h3) to give the guide an editorial, travel-magazine feel.
  • Source Sans 3 (Sans-Serif): Applied to body paragraphs, navigation links, lists, and form controls for clean readability on mobile and desktop screens.

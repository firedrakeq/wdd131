Enugu Trails & Heritage Guide

Website Planning Document | Quentin Chime | WDD 131

1. Site Name

Enugu Trails — Named after Enugu ("Coal City"), surrounded by hills, waterfalls, and historic landmarks.

2. Site Purpose

A digital guide to Enugu State's natural attractions, cultural sites, and local cuisine, featuring interactive filters, visitor tips, and a trip budget estimator.

3. Scenarios

  • Which waterfalls, caves, or hills (e.g., Awhum Waterfall, Ngwo Pine Forest) are open for weekend visits, and what are their entry fees?
  • Where can visitors find authentic local dishes like Okpa and Abacha near the city center?
  • What is the estimated budget for a small group on a one-day guided nature tour around Enugu?

4. Color Scheme

Primary Forest Green (#1B4332) Header, footer, primary buttons, and section headings.
Terracotta Gold (#B45309) Interactive highlights, badges, and accent borders.
Soft Mint Surface (#F0FDF4) Card backgrounds against a white (#FFFFFF) page background.

5. Typography

  • Merriweather (Serif): Headings (h1, h2, h3).
  • Source Sans 3 (Sans-Serif): Body paragraphs, lists, and navigation links.
